# Co-Creation / Participatory Design

A workshop where the people affected are genuine *co-designers*, not consulted subjects. "Co-creation" is heavily abused: most of it is a pre-decided answer run past a room whose stickies are admired and ignored. The move people skip is the honest one — naming whether real power actually moves, and equalising the room so non-experts contribute as equals.

## The recipe

1. **Name your rung (Arnstein's ladder) — honestly.** Participation ranges from *manipulation/therapy* (nonparticipation) through *informing/consultation/placation* (tokenism) to *partnership/delegated/citizen control* (real power). Most "co-creation" sits in tokenism. If the outcome is pre-decided, **don't call it co-creation.** Genuine co-creation means contributions can actually change the result.
2. **Treat participants as partners** — "experts of their own experience" who co-generate, not subjects who supply data (Sanders & Stappers). Your role shifts to orchestrating a collective process.
3. **Use generative make-tools** — collages, mapping, mockups (`tool-methods.md`). Asking only reaches *explicit* knowledge; *making* reaches tacit and latent knowledge ("they can put it in a picture, then find the word") — and equalises the room by moving off articulate-speech, where professionals dominate.
4. **Equalise structurally** — safety first (`tool-group-dynamics.md`); design-by-doing over discussion; write-first and small groups to stop dominant professionals and the loudest participants.
5. **Close the loop** — show participants how their input changed the outcome, or you've taught them it was extraction. Follow-through with an accountability edge.

## Required moves

- [ ] **Rung named honestly** — not consultation dressed as co-creation.
- [ ] **Participants as partners**, not data sources.
- [ ] **Generative make-tools used** — making, not just asking.
- [ ] **Power equalised structurally** — safety, making, write-first, small groups.
- [ ] **The loop closed** — input visibly changed the result; not extraction.

## Failure modes

- **Tokenism dressed as co-creation** — a predetermined outcome (Arnstein rungs 3–5). The dominant failure.
- **Extraction** — harvesting ideas into a closed process with no accountability back.
- **Asking, not making** — only explicit knowledge surfaces; professionals dominate.
- **Dominant professionals / loudest user** — vocabulary and standing crowd out the rest.
- **Facilitator with the answer** — a legitimation exercise for a decision already made.
