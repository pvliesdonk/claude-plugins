# Ideation (Write-First Generation)

The single highest-leverage habit in workshop ideation: **make people think alone before they think together.**

## Why open-floor brainstorming underperforms
A group calling out ideas generates *fewer* and *less original* ideas than the same number of people generating alone and pooling afterward (nominal groups; Taylor 1958, Diehl & Stroebe 1987). The dominant cause is **production blocking** — only one person can speak at a time, so others forget or suppress ideas while waiting. (Evaluation apprehension and social loafing add to it.) This is *not* "brainstorming is useless" — it's that the *verbal-group* format is the problem. The fix is structural.

## The move: generate in parallel, in writing, then share
- **1-2-4-All** (the default): alone (1 min) → pairs (2) → quartets (5) → whole group. Everyone thinks before anyone speaks; the loud few can't dominate.
- **Brainwriting / 6-3-5**: people write ideas on sheets and pass them — parallel, no turn-taking bottleneck.
- **Nominal Group Technique**: silent generation → round-robin posting → clarify → private vote. Reliable when you need fair generation *and* a ranking with mixed seniority.
- Keep Osborn's rules *for the generation phase*: defer judgment, welcome wild ideas, go for quantity, build on others. **Separate generation from evaluation** — diverge fully before converging.

## Divergent-thinking techniques (to spur ideas)
- **SCAMPER** — a prompt checklist (Substitute, Combine, Adapt, Modify, Put to other uses, Eliminate, Reverse) to break fixation mid-divergence.
- **The Double Diamond** — diverge/converge twice: first on the *problem* (don't skip it — you'll solve the wrong one), then on the *solution*.
- Lateral-thinking devices (random word, provocation) are useful *prompts* — present them as heuristics, not validated method.

## Failure modes
- **The open-floor brainstorm** — maximal production blocking; the loudest three talk.
- **Judging while generating** — kills the wild ideas.
- **Anchoring on the first idea** — no silent generation, so the first thing said frames everyone.
- **Solving the wrong problem** — skipping the first diamond.
