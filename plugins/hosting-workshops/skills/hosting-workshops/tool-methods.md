# The Methods Library

A method is a *microstructure* — a small, repeatable way of arranging who talks to whom, for how long — dropped into a beat of the arc to do one job. The skill is matching the method to the beat (open / explore / close), not knowing many. A one-trick host fits the room to a habit; a library fits the method to the work.

## Microstructures (normal-sized rooms)
- **1-2-4-All** — alone → pair → quartet → all. The default whenever you'd "open it to the group": includes everyone, kills domination structurally, ~15 min.
- **Affinity diagramming (KJ)** — cluster sticky notes by natural affinity *before* naming the groups; let themes emerge bottom-up (don't sort into pre-decided boxes). The workhorse "explore" method after a divergent burst.
- **Fishbowl** — inner circle discusses, outer observes; membership rotates. For a real debate in a big room or across a hierarchy.
- **The retrospective shape** — Set the Stage → Gather Data → Generate Insights → Decide → Close, under a blame-free Prime Directive.
- **Icebreakers & energizers** — *functional*, not filler: an icebreaker is a low-stakes first contribution that builds safety and speeds forming; an energizer re-activates attention mid-session. A forced-disclosure or embarrassing one does the opposite — keep them relevant.

## Large-group formats (dozens+)
- **World Café** — rotating small-table rounds on a question; ideas cross-pollinate; harvest the patterns. To *think together* at scale.
- **Open Space** — no pre-set agenda; participants post topics and self-organise (Law of Two Feet). For an open theme with expertise in the room. (See `large-group.md`.)

## How to choose
Name the beat — open, explore, or close — then pick for it, not by habit. Build a *range* across all three beats so you actually have a choice.

## Failure modes
- **The one-trick host** — every session is the same format.
- **"Opening it to the group"** — plenary free-for-all; the loud three talk.
- **Top-down "affinity"** — sorting into pre-decided buckets, confirming your assumptions.
- **The embarrassing icebreaker** — erodes the safety it was meant to build.
