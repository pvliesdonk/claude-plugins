# The Session Arc

Design the session from its *end*, and shape it as a diamond, not a line. Two ideas: **backward design** (start from the outcome) and the **diamond of participation** (widen before you narrow).

## Backward design
- **Outcome → evidence → activities, in that order.** Name one outcome; name the tangible artifact that proves it ("what exists at 5pm?"); then choose only activities that produce that artifact. Cut the fun activity that goes nowhere.
- Pitch the objective at the right level — an *apply / decide / create* verb, not "understand" (which you can't observe).

## The diamond
A group moving from problem to decision passes through three zones (Kaner): **divergent** (open up perspectives) → **groan zone** (the confused, frictional middle where different views collide) → **convergent** (narrow to shared ground), then **close**. Compressed: **open → diverge → converge → close.**
- The **groan zone is structural, not failure.** Name it ("this confusion is normal — it means we're doing the real work"), hold the process, don't rescue it with a premature answer. Most inexperienced facilitators fail here.
- **Don't converge before you've diverged.** Premature convergence = a shallow decision that reopens later.

## Agenda, timing, energy
- **Timebox and hold it** — fixed time per activity, a visible clock, a parking lot for tangents. One overrun eats the close.
- **Sequence to the energy curve** — high-cognitive divergent work in the morning peak; interactive, on-your-feet work in the **post-lunch dip** (~14:00–16:00, real); never heavy passive content there.
- **Vary the mode; ignore the attention-span myth** — the "people zone out after 10 minutes" claim isn't evidence-based; sameness loses a room, not a clock. Breaks (~every 90 min) are integration time, not the thing you cut.

## Scoping & pre-work
One clear objective; the minimum necessary people; pre-work done in advance so the room is spent *working*, not briefing; a stated non-scope to defend against creep.

## Failure modes
- **Activity-first design** — exercises chosen before the outcome; pleasant and pointless.
- **Premature convergence / panicking in the groan zone** — ramming a decision or letting it dissolve.
- **Timebox collapse** — one discussion eats the day; no close, no decision.
