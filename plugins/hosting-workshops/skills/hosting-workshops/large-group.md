# Large-Group Formats

When the room is large — dozens to hundreds — most microstructures break and the default failure is a podium with a passive audience. The large-group formats solve this counter-intuitively: *loosen* control and let the group organise itself. The move people skip is trusting the format — and staffing the harvest.

## Pick the format to the goal

- **World Café** — explore *a question* at scale. Small tables of four, timed ~20-min rounds, most people rotate while one "table host" stays to brief newcomers; ideas cross-pollinate; a final **harvest** surfaces patterns. Seven design principles, the load-bearing two: *explore questions that matter* and *listen together for patterns*. Use it to make a big group **think together**, not to make one decision.
- **Open Space Technology** — an *open theme* with the expertise in the room. **No pre-set agenda**: participants post topics to a marketplace wall and self-organise. Four principles + the **Law of Two Feet** (leave anywhere you're neither learning nor contributing). The principles are a release for the *facilitator's* need to control. Use when you trust the group to surface what matters.

## The recipe

1. **Choose by goal** — Café for a question; Open Space for an open theme; **neither** for a bounded decision (use a tight arc instead).
2. **Write a question that matters** — both formats live or die on it; pro-forma questions go flat.
3. **Design the container, then let go** — set theme, space, timing; resist scripting content. The engagement comes from the release of control.
4. **Staff the harvest** — decide in advance how table-host notes / session reports get captured and synthesised. That's the deliverable.
5. **Don't fake it** — if you have a predetermined answer, don't run Open Space; the format exposes it and burns trust.

## Required moves

- [ ] **Format matched to goal** — not a Café when you needed a decision.
- [ ] **A real, compelling question** at the centre.
- [ ] **Container set, content released** — not scripted minute-by-minute.
- [ ] **Harvest staffed and planned** — energy captured, not lost.
- [ ] **Genuinely open** — no hidden predetermined outcome.

## Failure modes

- **Open Space with a hidden agenda** — participants feel the manipulation instantly.
- **No harvest** — great energy, paper tablecloths, nothing captured.
- **Wrong format for the goal** — a Café when a bounded decision was needed.
- **An under-sized question** — flat conversations.
- **Sponsor panic** — a convener who can't tolerate the loss of control collapses the self-organisation.
